# Settings

[C18] Its own window (in the Dock and Alt+Tab while open), opened with **Settings…** (⌘,) from More, the menu bar or tray. A sidebar of pages runs under the window buttons, as in System Settings. Every change applies at once and reaches an open picker.

- **Window**: 920 × 560 by default, minimum 800 × 400, each limited to the screen's work area less a margin, so a small or scaled screen (1440 × 900 at `GDK_SCALE=2` is 720 × 450) never puts a control off it. A two-column shell with a reserved `--titlebar-height` (macOS only, where the window buttons sit over the content); the sidebar background runs full height. Nothing slides behind the title bar; only the page and the sidebar list scroll.
- **Pages** (in this order): **General** (opening shortcut, Open at Login, pasting), **Appearance** (one live picture, then Appearance, Accent, Rows, Window size and Text size), **Libraries**, **Citations** (your format per kind, remembered apps with Forget, writings only when any exist, Recent citations and Open Settings File), **Shortcuts**, then after a gap **Advanced** and **About Ibis**. A page with no built controls stays out of the sidebar.
- **Only built settings appear**: no disabled promises or "Coming soon" rows, and nothing unbuilt in the search index.
- Each setting is one entry in one registry (key, page, group, label, description, control, default, search words); pages and search are drawn from it.
- **Groups and rows** [D17]: `ui/Layout` sections of `Group` cards (`--r-large`, `--surface-grouped`) of `Row`s: a label, an optional description, one control at the end. Cards keep their natural height; rows grow with text; controls move below a label that does not fit. No page opens with more than eight rows; rarer rows go behind **Details…**, a disclosure row in its own card whose chevron turns down while its rows show. Every group but a page's first lone row has a title (General: **Behavior**, **Pasting**).
- A setting that differs from its default shows **Use Default** (`reset`, ↺) with Undo
  right after the setting's name [C24], on the leading side, so every control in
  every row and table ends on the card's one trailing edge and nothing is reserved
  while the setting is default. Restoring a default never opts into background network use. Settings and `settings.json` show the
  same saved choices. A valid file edit applies live, retires an older Undo
  and updates open controls; a problem stays visible and refuses saves until
  repaired.
- **Search** (decision 0047, maintainer, 2026-11-12) heads the sidebar; ⌘F / Ctrl+F focuses it. It uses the palette’s folded, every-word matching over rendered row definitions and localized synonyms ("dark mode", "startup"); results replace the page list, grouped by page. ↑↓ move and Enter or a click opens the page and any containing **Details…**, scrolls only the page, briefly fades the shared highlight fill and focuses the control. Esc clears, then leaves search. No match says **No settings match ‘…’** with the query. "ibis" or "thoth" opens About.
- A page opens at its top with a `--type-display-*` heading that stays put while the page scrolls under it, as System Settings keeps its title in the toolbar; a hairline appears under it once anything has scrolled beneath. Clicking the current page in the sidebar returns it to the top.
- **Appearance** (maintainer, 2026-11-10, option C) is one group: **one live picture** of the picker at the top, the real picker scaled (`ui/PictureChoice`'s preview), answering "what will this look like?" for every choice under it, then one plain control per row: Appearance (System, Light, Dark as three small thumbnails, System split diagonally; Ibis keeps its own choice), Accent, Rows, Window size and Text size as segmented controls. The picture takes the chosen window's shape, rows and theme.
- **Previews** (`settings/PickerPicture`, `ui/PictureChoice`): a preview is the real picker at one window shape, scaled (`--preview-window`), so every picture on the page reads alike; a picture choice inside a card draws the picture and its ring, never a second box.
- **Title bar**: only macOS draws its window buttons over Settings, so only there does the sidebar leave `--titlebar-height` above its search field; elsewhere it keeps `--inset`.
- **Accent**: a segmented choice of **Follow the Mac** (the default) and **Ibis scarlet**. No other hues, free colour picker, custom fonts, themes or CSS.
- A failed save shows an alert and restores the saved choice; queued choices finish saving (at most two seconds) before the window closes. Its **Use Default** is named with its setting for assistive technology and says "Use Default" on hover; a confirmed reset offers eight-second Undo [D16]. File choices have no default. Automatic update checks reset to off; window-size Undo preserves Custom dimensions with a native revision ticket.

**Shortcuts.** Settings › Shortcuts has a search field, then grouped cards in one order (`SHORTCUT_GROUPS`: Open Ibis, While searching, In the citation, Your library, Anywhere in Ibis, In a menu); the keys sheet shows the same groups, named the same, in the same order, less In a menu, which every menu shares. Open Ibis holds one row, "From any app", with the opening shortcut. Only the opening-shortcut recorder edits a key; fixed keys are plain secondary text at body size, as menus write key equivalents, in full ink under Increase Contrast. Group headings scroll with their cards, never sticking over a card's edge. Keys and the "or" between them share one baseline with equal gaps. General and Shortcuts share `ui/ShortcutSetting`. The Keyboard Shortcuts sheet (⌘/) uses `ui/Sheet` and the shared window frame [C13]. Its body has two equal columns of groups, folding to one when they no longer fit; Back or Esc returns to the picker.

- The recorder shows the shortcut in system notation; clicking says **Type a shortcut…**; Esc, Tab or leaving cancels.
- A shortcut needs Control, Option or Command; **⌘ + one key is refused**, as are picker commands and, on Windows, Ctrl+Alt with a printable key (AltGr). Refusals name the owner and keep the working shortcut.
- **Use Default** appears only for a different shortcut. On Wayland the desktop owns the shortcut: show its trigger text verbatim or **Not assigned**, with **Change…** where the portal supports it.

**Permissions.** A guide in Settings › General › Pasting says why in one sentence, names the system's list in its current words, shows a picture of the switch, opens the pane with one button, notices the change by itself and confirms in words. Mac auto-paste needs PostEvent and Accessibility; Settings says allowed only after both read back. The Ibis icon beside **Open System Settings** can be dragged into the list ("If Ibis is not in the list, drag it there."). Granting never replays a paste: return to the document, open Ibis again and press Enter.

**Citations › Cite from your editor.** A switch, off by default. On, it shows the address to paste into the editor's Cite as You Write URL setting, with **Copy Address**, and says who can use it: editors on this computer, never web pages. While an editor waits, the picker's notices say "Citing for your editor in Pandoc" and the preview is in that format; Enter sends it, Esc answers with nothing. The format palette, paste and the app question step aside for that one citation.

**General.** **Open at Login** is off until turned on and read from the system, never `settings.json`. **Bird facts and milestones** closes the same card. **Show in menu bar** is on by default; while it is off, opening Ibis from Applications opens Settings. The menu bar icon is a one-colour bird from `brand/` opening a native menu (maintainer, 2026-11-11): **Open Ibis** with the global shortcut as its key equivalent (the actual key on the active layout; a character Tauri cannot represent stays in the label); a separator; the library name · reference count (**No library yet** before choosing), inline checked choices for Zotero when detected or in use and the last chosen file by filename, then **Choose File…**; a separator; **Settings…**, **Check for Updates…**, **Help**; a separator; **Quit Ibis** (the nightly names itself). Checks follow the library actually open, including while another choice opens or fails. A failed library names the problem in one plain sentence and puts its one fix directly underneath: updates for a newer Zotero database, Choose File for a missing file, Try Again for a failed refresh. Move an existing action instead of repeating it. Files are watched, so there is no Reload Library item. Automatic checks and Open at Login live only in Settings. Help groups Keyboard Shortcuts (Settings › Shortcuts), What’s New (release notes), a separator, Report a Problem…, Suggest an Idea…, a separator, Reset Window Position, a separator, and the full build, which copies on click. Native items update in place; only changed choices or action order require insertion/removal. On Windows left-click opens the picker and right-click opens the menu; AppIndicator on Linux may open the menu on either click, with Open Ibis first. Muda offers no alternate-item API, so there is no ⌥ alternate; `brand/` has no attention-dot bird, so the icon stays unchanged.

**The ibis command.** On macOS and Linux, General offers **Install the ibis
command** once as a persistent row, without repeated prompts. It creates a
per-user `~/.local/bin/ibis` symlink to the bundled command; AppImage uses an
owned copy there so the command survives unmounting. Linux packages register
nothing on PATH as root. Windows installers add the command to the user's PATH.
Foreign commands are preserved. The row shows not installed, the installed
version matching this app, a different installed version, or the location of
another `ibis` (version unavailable). **Remove Command** removes only Ibis-owned
artifacts. If `~/.local/bin` is absent from PATH on macOS or Linux, show the exact
shell-profile line with **Copy**; no elevation or profile editing. Refresh only on a visit or
window focus, never while hidden. All controls use `ui/Layout` and `ui/Button`.

**Another computer.** Settings › General’s **Details…** holds **Back up settings** (Back Up…, a save dialog that starts in iCloud Drive on macOS, OneDrive on Windows, otherwise Documents) and **Restore settings** (Restore…). A chosen backup is checked whole, then the row lists every change as "Setting: before → after" with notes (a bibliography not on this computer, a shortcut kept for this system), **Cancel** and the primary **Restore and Restart**. Nothing changes before that button; a bibliography shows by its file name only. Refusals say why in one line and change nothing ([moving to a new computer](../moving-to-a-new-computer.md)).

**About** (maintainer, 2026-11-10, option C). One centred column, as System Settings' About: the actual app icon, the name, a **Version x** button that copies it (⌥ shows and copies the full build), **Check for Updates** under the version, as Mac apps place it, with its result under it, then the mission line and the line on the name, which keeps the room of two lines so a new fact never moves the page. Under it, the Updates group (one row: the automatic-checks switch) is the same card as every page, and the links and the free-software note are centred at the foot. The mission line is the hero's lead, in the text colour; the line on the name is secondary. The icon is the page's only bird. It fits 920 × 560 without scrolling. **Check for Updates…** in the tray and menu bar checks at once and leaves Settings closed (maintainer, 2026-11-07): an update found is the offer (install now or later); otherwise one native prompt says Ibis has the newest version, could not check, is already checking, or does not update itself. Automatic update offers never show or focus the picker; they wait for empty, idle input, and **Later** is the default for every key (decision 0019).
