Everything the picker does, the ibis command does too, from the same engine.

The examples run against sample.bib, three invented references, and show exactly what Ibis prints.

@terminal

Search and cite from a terminal

{{terminal}}

Ibis prints to standard output and errors to standard error; it never copies by itself. Pipe it to pbcopy, clip or wl-copy to copy.
