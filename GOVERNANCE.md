# Governance

Ibis is a free, open-source project. It is and will remain free of charge, without ads, telemetry, paid tiers or closed editions.

## Today

The project has a single maintainer, who has final say on changes and releases. Decisions with lasting effect are recorded as Architecture Decision Records in `docs/adr/`, so anyone can see what was decided and why.

## As the project grows

- When at least three people contribute regularly, the maintainer will invite them to form a maintainers group that decides by consensus, falling back to a simple majority.
- The GitHub organization will always have at least two owners once a second trusted maintainer exists, so the project can continue if one person steps away.
- If the project receives money, it will be held by a non-profit fiscal host (for example Open Source Collective) with public accounts.

## Why "free forever" is credible

- The code is licensed AGPL-3.0-or-later. Anyone who distributes a modified version, or runs one as a network service, must share their source.
- Contributions are made under the Developer Certificate of Origin, not a Contributor License Agreement. No single party can relicense everyone's work as closed source.
- The name and logo are protected only to prevent confusion (see `TRADEMARKS.md`), never to restrict free use.
